# Past Materials Archive of School of Internet, Anhui University

## Preface

This repository is voluntarily collected, organized, and maintained by students of the School of Internet, Anhui University. It aims to compile and share past exam papers, study outlines, and related materials for various courses. We hope this continuously updated resource will help students better grasp key course concepts, prepare for exams efficiently, and foster a collaborative and progressive learning atmosphere.
